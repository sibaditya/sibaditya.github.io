01.About Me

I'm an Android engineer and tech lead based in Milton, Canada. I've spent 12+ years building and shipping high-performance apps on Google Play, and I'm hands-on in Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines and Flow, with Clean Architecture (MVVM/MVI) and strong testing habits.

At Bell Canada I own the Android roadmap for My Bell, Virgin Plus, My Lucky Mobile and My PC Mobile, and lead a team of 8 engineers while staying in the code: building features, reviewing PRs and setting Android standards.

Beyond my day job I run Hazelcrow, a solutions lab where I design, build and publish my own apps and help others ship theirs. That means Docsmith, a document scanner and PDF toolkit, and the Satsang Canada apps and website. I handle the whole path to market: Play Billing, AdMob, ML Kit, FCM, localization and Play Console releases.

I document decisions clearly and use spec-driven development with modern AI coding assistants and LLM tooling to move faster without lowering quality. Earlier roles include BlackBerry Messenger, Paytm, Flipp, Aspiring Minds and Samsung Research Institute.

04.Projects & Hazelcrow

Personal product · Android Live on Google Play

Docsmith: Document Scanner & PDF Toolkit

A free Android app to scan, sign, fill in, mark up, split and merge documents. No account, no cloud: files stay on the phone.

  • Scanning with Google ML Kit and a custom perspective-correction editor for adjusting page corners; OCR text extraction.
  • PDF merge and split, page reordering, form filling, annotations and handwritten signatures. Signed pages keep an unsigned copy so signatures can be removed or redone.
  • Shared logic, storage and state in a Kotlin Multiplatform module (MVI stores, Okio file I/O) so an iOS target can reuse the same code.
  • Monetized with AdMob (interstitial, rewarded, native), an ad-free purchase through Play Billing and Google UMP consent for GDPR. Interstitials are limited by a free-share allowance and cooldown.
  • Localized into English, Spanish, French and Hindi. Handled release engineering: Gradle/AGP/Kotlin/Compose upgrades, R8 shrinking, signed AAB builds and versioned releases.
